Problem & solution

The problem

People with vision impairment rely on tools like VoiceOver, TalkBack and Be My Eyes. They are reactive: they wait to be asked, and everyday tasks stay slow and tiring.

The solution

VoiceTouch is a voice-first AI layer that anticipates, automates and acts across navigation, reading, tasks and safety, all in one experience.

Key decisions

Adaptive onboarding

Priya wants depth and speed. James wants simplicity and safety. Three voice questions in the first 60 seconds detect the user type and tailor the experience.

10 free camera reads a day

Reading physical objects was the number one pain point. The free limit creates the “I need Pro” moment once the user already depends on the feature. Free is ₹0, Pro is ₹299 a month.

Emergency trigger

Voice is the only requirement, from start to finish.

  1. Say “Emergency” or hold any physical button for 3 seconds.
  2. A 5-second window opens. Say “Cancel” to stop it.
  3. If not cancelled, a loud alarm fires and two contacts get an SMS with live location.
  4. A contact can mark the alert resolved from a link in the SMS.
  5. Say “I’m safe” to stop the alarm.
