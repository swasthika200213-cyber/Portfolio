Case study

Arosx.ai

A landing page for a multi-language sales automation platform that helps small businesses capture, qualify and manage leads on WhatsApp.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Tools

Figma

Deliverable

Landing page
arosx.ai

Scroll inside the window, or use the buttons to jump to a section.

01

The page’s job

The problem

Small businesses get enquiries on WhatsApp all day. Many go unanswered, unqualified or forgotten, so revenue quietly leaks out of the chat.

The page

Explain in seconds what Arosx.ai does, prove it with a look at the product, and move a visitor from curious to “Get Early Access”.

02

How the page is built

The product, shown in the hero

A phone mockup of a real chat, with floating cards for the lead score, potential revenue, language and scheduled follow-up. One image explains what the product does.

Problem before product

A short flow (incoming message, no reply, lost lead, lost revenue) and five pain cards make the visitor feel the problem before the solution appears.

Multi-language as a visual

Scripts from several languages orbit one central icon, so the headline feature is understood without reading a word.

Proof and a clear pricing choice

A dashboard preview, a comparison with traditional WhatsApp tools, and a three-plan pricing table with the middle plan highlighted help the visitor decide.

Trust

A founder section with a photo and short story, and an “MSME Registered Startup” mark in the hero, make a new product feel credible.
